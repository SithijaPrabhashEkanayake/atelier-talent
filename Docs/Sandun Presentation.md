# **Research Background**

- Rapid advancement of **Information and Communication Technology**

  - **(ICT)** has transformed global creative industries

- Fashion, film, pageantry, and entertainment increasingly rely on **digital**

  - **platforms** for recruitment and visibility

- Despite this growth, many regions still depend on **fragmented, informal**

  - **social media networks**

- Freelance models and creative talent face **limited professional exposure**

**and verification challenges**

## **Research Background**

- Recruiters struggle with **fraudulent profiles, inefficient talent scouting,**

**and high administrative workload**

- Existing platforms are often **agency-restricted or region-specific** ,

- excluding independent talent

- There is a growing need for a **secure, centralized, and globally**

#### **accessible digital ecosystem**

- This study responds to the **trust gap and accessibility issues** in current

  - creative industry recruitment systems

## **Problem Justification**

 **Study Focus:**

Development of a **Global Multidimensional Talent Marketplace and Casting Management System.**

 **Purpose:**

To provide a **centralized, secure platform** connecting freelance models, creative talent, and recruiters globally.

####  **Reason:**

Current systems are fragmented, agency-focused, and often unreliable, making talent discovery and verification difficult

## **Problem Justification**

##### **Issue**

Fragmented Platforms

Fake Profiles

Limited Access

High Admin Workload

**Current Situation**

Talent and recruiters use scattered social media Unverified social media accounts

Most platforms are agency or region-specific Recruiters manually check profiles and portfolios

**Impact / Problem** Hard to find verified talent, slow casting process Recruiters risk fraud; models’ credibility affected Independent talent gets excluded from opportunities Inefficient and timeconsuming

## **Research Question/s and Objective/s**

- The research question of this study is:

- **How can a secure and centralized digital platform improve visibility, verification, and access for freelance models and creative talent while making recruitment more efficient for recruiters globally?**

- The aims of this study are:

- To design a **centralized platform** for models, creative talent, and recruiters

- To ensure to reduce fraud **talent verification and secure profiles**

- To improve **accessibility and visibility** of freelance talent worldwide

- To make the and **talent discovery and recruitment process efficient**

- reliable

- Provides a **central platform** for models, creative talent, and

  - recruiters worldwide

- Helps **freelance talent gain visibility** and verified professional profiles

- Reduces **fraud and fake profiles** , increasing trust in

  - recruitment

- Makes **casting and talent selection faster and easier**

- Bridges **geographical gaps** , allowing global collaboration

- Supports **industry growth** by connecting more talent with opportunities

## **Literature Review**

- Social media is widely used for talent discovery, but **unverified profiles and fragmented**

  - **systems** create trust issues (Lee, 2019)

- ICT has **transformed creative industries** , improving accessibility and efficiency (Smith

  - & Johnson, 2020)

- Existing talent platforms are often **agency-only or region-specific** , limiting freelance access (Kumar & Singh, 2021)

- Verification systems improve **credibility and trust** for both talent and recruiters (Garcia, 2018)

**Key Takeaway:** Platforms exist but **lack global reach, verification, and inclusivity** .

## **Literature Review**

- **Challenges identified in literature:**  Fraud and fake profiles

- Limited access for independent talent

- Inefficient recruitment processes

- Geographical fragmentation (Adams et al., 2022; Chen, 2020)

**Gap:** No secure, centralized, globally accessible platform exists for freelance creative talent

**This study addresses:**

- •Talent verification and trust

- •Global accessibility

- •Efficient and reliable recruitment

### **Proposed Methodology**

##### **Research Approach:**

- **Conceptual Framework:**

**Mixed-Method** (Quantitative + Qualitative)

- Quantitative: Surveys, analytics

- Qualitative: Interviews, focus groups

##### **Types of Data:**

- **Independent Variables:** Verification, Accessibility, Usability, Global Reach

- **Dependent Variables:** Talent Visibility, Recruitment Efficiency, User Trust

- **Primary Data:** Surveys, interviews, user testing, observations

- **Secondary Data:** Articles, industry reports, online platform analytics

###### **Sources of Data:**

- Freelance models & creative talent

- Recruiters & agencies

- Platform users & industry experts

- **Outcome:** Successful Talent Matching

- **Hypotheses:**

- H1: Verification → Increased Trust

- H2: Global Access → Increased Visibility

- H3: Usability → Improved Recruitment Efficiency

- H4: Centralized Platform → Better Talent Matching

## **Data collection and analysis**

##### **Population and Sample**

- The study focuses on a selected group relevant to the research topic. A sample will be chosen using an appropriate sampling method. Only participants who meet the study criteria will be included.

##### **Data Collection Method**

- Data will be collected using **surveys / interviews** with a structured questionnaire or interview guide.

##### **Procedure**

- Participants will be selected, informed consent will be obtained, and data will be collected within a set time period.

##### **Data Analysis Method**

- Data will be analyzed using **statistical or thematic analysis** , with the support of software

## **Scope of the study**

**Scope of the Study (In-Scope / Out-of-Scope) In-Scope**

- Participants who are directly relevant to the research topic

- A limited sample size as defined by the study design

- Participants from a specific geographical location

- Data collected within the defined study period

- **Out-of-Scope**

- Individuals outside the selected geographical location

- Participants who do not meet the inclusion criteria

- Broader populations beyond the selected sample

- Data collected outside the specified timeframe
